BLOCKERS FOR SUBMIT: Floor, Criticality. Everything else EMPTY just leaves a dropdown empty.

ORDER TO CREATE THE EMPTY ONES (UI, Master Data Management menu; route /master-data-management/<slug>)
 1. Organization > Building / Floor / Zone   (building-floor-zone)  add Floor(s) under "industry development"   [required]
 2. Organization > Building / Floor / Zone   add Zone(s) under the floor                                      [optional]
 3. Organization > Location                  (location)  rooms under building/floor/zone                      [optional]
 4. Asset > Criticality                      (criticality)  e.g. Critical, High, Medium, Low, Non-Critical    [required]
 5. Asset > Condition                        (condition)  e.g. New, Good, Fair, Poor
 6. Asset > Manufacturer                     (manufacturer)  must exist before models
 7. Asset > Asset Model                      (asset-model)  pick a manufacturer for each model
 8. Vendor > Supplier                        (supplier)
 9. People > Employee                        (employee)  add custodians in department "industry development",
                                             and edit Ajithkumar R -> department "industry development"
10. People > Team                            (team)  department "industry development"                         [optional]
Optional (already usable): Asset > Category / Subcategory - add a subcategory under "subelector";
Assets > Asset Types & Models (/assets/types-models) - add asset types under subelector/subelec.
The registration form's Asset Type list comes from Assets > Asset Types & Models, not from MDM > Asset > Asset Type.
Statuses, tagging methods and lifecycle stages are fixed lists in code, so nothing to create.
